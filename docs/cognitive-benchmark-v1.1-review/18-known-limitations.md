# Known Limitations

1. Kimi K2.6 temperature is provider-controlled; repeatability is not temperature-zero determinism.
2. Adapter v2.1 recovered two schema-validation failures; reliability evidence is Development-scale, not a formal statistical guarantee.
3. Retrieval-Only outscored Full Omni, especially on Conflict Resolution and Cross-Agent Transfer; this requires product-level investigation.
4. Full Omni Cross-Agent Provenance and Invalidated Fact Rejection both remain 0.
5. Secondary Agent Review flagged many score issues and is not independent from the DeepSeek Answer model or equivalent to human review.
6. Kimi K2.6 and extraction dollar costs remain unpriced in the estimate because verified pinned rates/usage are absent.
7. Physical deletion and memory compression remain not implemented and excluded from aggregate scoring.
