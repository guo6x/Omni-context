The frozen Benchmark commit, Development dataset, scenario IDs, Answer/Judge prompts, config, scoring version, answer schema, Kimi adapter, and rubric were verified compatible. No Memory and Retrieval-Only frozen results were therefore technically reusable.

They were not combined with Candidate v3 because Development-35 was not authorized. A 7-scenario targeted score is not comparable to the frozen 35-scenario Full Omni score, so no misleading delta is reported.
