# ARR October 2026 — Service Contributor Plan

**Status date:** 2026-10-07  
**Submission deadline:** 2026-10-12 AoE  
**Contributor registration deadline:** 2026-10-14 AoE (48h after submission deadline)

## Current policy

Starting with the October 2026 ARR cycle, review capacity is capped.

A submission is **guaranteed review only if it nominates one qualified designated service contributor**.

If no qualified contributor is nominated, the paper may still be submitted, but it enters the unsupported-submission lottery and may be desk rejected if reviewer capacity is exhausted.

The designated service contributor:

- may be an author or a **non-author researcher**;
- does **not** need to be qualified in the same ARR track as the paper;
- may support at most **2 submissions** in the same ARR cycle;
- must be familiar with the paper;
- must explicitly vouch that the paper is ready for consideration at a top-tier conference;
- must have an accurate OpenReview profile;
- must complete the contributor registration form by 48h after the submission deadline;
- accepts real review/chair service obligations and is responsible for arranging a qualified replacement if an emergency prevents service.

Do **not** add somebody as a paper author merely to satisfy this requirement. ARR explicitly permits a qualified non-author designated contributor.

## Qualification rules

Automatic reviewer qualification currently requires:

### PhD / postdoc / faculty path

At least 2 publications in approved major CL / Findings / relevant venues.

### PhD student or industry researcher with completed Master's

At least 3 publications in approved venues.

Bachelor's and Master's students do not automatically qualify by publication record alone.

### Manual verification path

Possible for researchers with sufficient verified reviewing experience.

Current paths include:

- 10+ high-quality reviews from ARR or listed major venues plus a completed Master's degree; or
- 10+ ARR secondary reviews with qualified supervision and a supervisor recommendation;
- for candidates without a completed Master's degree, the reviewing-based mentorship path requires the documented review record and supervisor recommendation.

Manual verification close to deadline is not guaranteed to be processed in time.

## Practical decision for this submission

Treat the author as **not currently qualified as the designated service contributor unless the ARR automated checker explicitly says otherwise**.

The preferred path for the October 2026 submission is therefore:

1. identify one qualified researcher who is willing to read the manuscript;
2. confirm that they are not already designated on 2 October-cycle submissions;
3. have them run the ARR qualification checker in OpenReview;
4. send them the anonymous review PDF and a concise evidence/reproducibility summary;
5. obtain explicit agreement that they are willing to vouch for the paper and perform ARR service;
6. nominate them as designated service contributor in the ARR submission;
7. immediately after the submission exists, have them open their OpenReview console and complete the registration form;
8. verify completion before the 2026-10-14 AoE cutoff.

## Candidate priority

Use this order:

1. faculty / postdoc / PhD researcher who already knows the work or has reviewed the manuscript;
2. a qualified NLP/ML researcher in the author's academic network;
3. a qualified industry researcher with relevant publications and a completed Master's degree;
4. only if no qualified contributor can be secured: submit without one and accept the lottery risk.

Do not:

- fabricate reviewing/publication history;
- nominate somebody who has not read enough of the paper to vouch for its quality;
- use a contributor who is already supporting 2 submissions;
- add a sham co-author;
- wait until after the submission deadline to begin contributor outreach.

## OpenReview profile hard gate

Every author and the designated service contributor must maintain a complete and accurate OpenReview profile, including:

- current and historical affiliations;
- career status;
- active email address;
- ORCID;
- conflicts of interest;
- DBLP / ACL Anthology links when applicable;
- accurate publication records for qualification checking.

## Go / no-go rule

**GO for ARR October 2026** if, before final submission:

- review PDF / supplement / checklist gates pass; and
- either a qualified contributor has explicitly agreed and can complete registration, or the author consciously accepts the lottery risk.

**NO-GO as a guaranteed-review submission** if no qualified contributor is secured.

In that case, preserve the frozen manuscript and choose between:

- submitting to the October cycle as a lottery-supported paper; or
- holding for the next ARR cycle while building a compliant service-contributor path.

No new scientific experiment is required to resolve this gate.
