This project used ChatGPT extensively for:

- research framing and manuscript restructuring;
- drafting and revising prose;
- literature-search assistance;
- reviewer-style critique;
- LaTeX conversion and figure-source generation;
- code / artifact inspection and consistency checking.

The human author(s) remain responsible for:

- the scientific claims;
- experimental design and frozen artifacts;
- every numerical result;
- citation verification;
- final wording and submission;
- compliance with venue policies.

ChatGPT must not be listed as an author.
